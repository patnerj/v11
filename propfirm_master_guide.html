2. Trading Engine & Market Simulation Mechanics

The core trading engine (FXSIM_Trading_Engine) replicates true institutional ECN broker conditions with exact margin checks, spreads, leverage tiers, directional PnL formulas, and automated tick-by-tick order fills.

Margin & Leverage Math

Margin required to open a position is calculated dynamically based on asset contract size, lot size, current ask price, and account leverage tier:

Margin Required = (Lot Size × Contract Size × Asset Price) / Leverage Tier

Examples:
• EURUSD (1 Lot, Price: 1.0850, Leverage 1:100):
  Margin = (1 × 100,000 × 1.0850) / 100 = $1,085.00
• XAUUSD / Gold (1 Lot = 100 oz, Price: $2,350.00, Leverage 1:100):
  Margin = (1 × 100 × 2,350.00) / 100 = $2,350.00
• BTCUSD (1 Lot = 1 Coin, Price: $65,000.00, Leverage 1:30):
  Margin = (1 × 1 × 65,000.00) / 30 = $2,166.67

Directional PnL Calculation

Floating and closed profit/loss are computed strictly according to order side and market tick movements:

BUY Orders:  PnL = (Current Bid Price - Open Price) × Lot Size × Contract Size - Commission - Swaps
SELL Orders: PnL = (Open Price - Current Ask Price) × Lot Size × Contract Size - Commission - Swaps

Pending Orders Engine & Slippage Simulation

The platform supports 4 types of pending orders with automatic execution on live price ticks:

Order Type Trigger Condition Execution Behavior
BUY_LIMIT Market Ask ≤ Target Price Fills when price drops to or below target (Discount Buy).
SELL_LIMIT Market Bid ≥ Target Price Fills when price rallies to or above target (Premium Sell).
BUY_STOP Market Ask ≥ Target Price Fills on bullish breakout above target price.
SELL_STOP Market Bid ≤ Target Price Fills on bearish breakdown below target price.
⚡ Instant Fill Guarantee
During market hours, pending orders are cross-checked every tick cycle via process_pending_orders(). Filled orders convert immediately into active positions with pre-set Stop Loss and Take Profit attached.

3. Institutional Risk Management & Compliance Shields

The platform features an advanced risk monitoring suite designed to protect firm capital while providing transparent rules to traders.

🛑 Mandatory Stop Loss Shield

• Open Block: Orders submitted without valid SL are instantly rejected with error code 422.
• Removal Guard: Modifying an open position to remove SL (setting SL to 0) is strictly blocked.

⏱️ Anti-Scalping 30s Shield

• Duration Check: Trades closed within < 30 seconds are intercepted.
• Action Modes: Configurable per plan (flag for investigation, void_pnl to zero profits, or reject closure).

📰 High-Impact News Event Guard

• Buffer Window: 5-minute pre/post red-folder news buffer.
• Global News Lock: Blocks opening new market orders during volatility spikes to prevent negative slippage exploits.

⚖️ Consistency Rule (50% Cap)

• Formula: Single-day profit cannot exceed 50% of total accumulated profit.
• Fair Evaluation: Ensures traders achieve targets through consistent strategy rather than lucky 1-candle gambles.

Syndicate Radar & Cross-Account Hedging Detection

The Syndicate Radar scans all open positions across different accounts in real-time. When two separate traders open opposite positions (e.g. Trader A BUY EURUSD 5 Lots, Trader B SELL EURUSD 5 Lots) within milliseconds of each other, the system logs a high-risk clustered incident into wp_fxsim_syndicate_clusters and alerts compliance officers.

Drawdown Models Comparison

Drawdown Model Calculation Mechanism Target Use Case
Static Drawdown Fixed dollar distance from initial balance (e.g. $100k account breaches at $90k). Standard Evaluation & Beginner Plans.
Daily Equity Drawdown Resets daily at 00:00 UTC based on higher of starting balance or equity (e.g. 5% Daily). 2-Step Evaluation Standard.
Trailing Equity Drawdown Trails the highest equity high-water mark; locks at starting balance once in profit. Instant Funding & Fast-Track.
Trailing Balance Drawdown Trails only closed balance high-water mark, ignoring unrealized floating gains. Swing Trader Friendly Plans.

Trader Payouts & Profit Split Lifecycle

Payout Flow:
1. Trader reaches payout eligibility date with positive balance and passed KYC.
2. Trader submits request specifying amount and method (USDT / Bank Wire).
3. Backend validates: Zero open positions + Available profit balance.
4. Profit Split Applied: 80% Trader Amount / 20% Firm Retained.
5. Admin approves -> TXN Hash logged -> Email receipt dispatched -> Balance reset.

# Run Sub-Minute Pending Orders & SL/TP Cross Engine (via curl)

* * * * * ( sleep 30; curl -s https://yourpropfirm.com/wp-json/fxsim/v1/cron/tick-check >/dev/null 2>&1 )

cd /var/www/propfirm/mt5-price-service
npm install
pm2 start server.js --name "propfirm-price-feed"
